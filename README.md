# Wordwild

A learn-to-read adventure for 5-year-olds (iPad and iPhone). Kids learn phonics with Pip, trace letters with a finger, hop along stepping stones to read words, build words, teach Pip, read decodable books and hatch Wordling eggs.

## How it teaches
- **Systematic synthetic phonics**, assuming letter names are known: 7 worlds, 34 lessons, 62 graphemes plus 42 heart (tricky) words.
  - Worlds 1–4 (Sunny Farm, Meadow Muddle, Duck Pond, Barn Loft): s a t p i n … ow oi.
  - World 5 (Cloud Coop): consonant blends, two-part words, y saying "ee", -ing.
  - World 6 (Seaside Shells): other spellings of known sounds (ay ou ea ie y oy ir er aw ew).
  - World 7 (Starry Night Roost): split vowels a‑e i‑e o‑e u‑e (shown as gold "magic e" tiles), wh, ph.
- **Decodable books and sentences**: 34 stories, 8 big books and about 160 sentences. `tools/build_content.mjs` refuses to build if any story, book, sentence or question answer uses a sound not yet taught (and flags words that only look decodable, such as of, give or snow).
- **Lesson loop**: spaced review → meet new sounds (hear it, find it, trace it with a finger) → bubble pop → hop blending (Pip hops along a stepping stone for each sound you tap, you slide to blend, pick the picture and Pip flaps over to it; tap sounds, slide to blend, pick the picture) → syllable chop (two-part words) → word forge (spelling with sound boxes) → heart words → a word game → teach Pip (spot Pip's mistake) → a sentence game → story + 2 or 3 questions → egg hatch.
  - Word games (taking turns by lesson): Memory Match (word ↔ picture), Word Ladder (change one sound: cat → cot → cog), Spelling Detective (which spelling of a sound is in this word; always used when a lesson adds a new spelling).
  - Sentence games: Sentence Builder (word tiles in order, with the capital and full stop), Silly or Sensible?, Fix It! (Pip's missing capital/full stop), Dictation (hear a sentence and write it: pick words, spell one with sound tiles, choose the end mark), Finish My Sentence (pick and spell the last word, then hear your sentence).
- **Adaptive**: each sound and word has a spaced-repetition box; difficulty (number of choices, distractors, bubble speed) tracks recent accuracy to keep it near 85%. Mix-ups (e.g. b/d) feed the next warm-up.
- **Story questions**: every story and big book asks 2 or 3 spoken questions after the last page. Answers are pictures or short decodable words, with exactly one right answer. At least one asks who, what or where; from World 3 on one asks "why", "how did they feel" or "what happens next". Results are saved by kind and by story (merge-safe) and shown in the grown-ups corner as "Understanding stories". Re-reading a book from the library does not count again.
- **Reading speed (grown-ups only)**: when a word is read and acted on (hop blending picture pick, Memory Match with the word card turned first, Speed Read in a lesson, Silly or Sensible), the time from the word appearing to the right answer is saved, not counting time while the voice is talking or the tab is hidden, and dropping values under 0.5 s or over 30 s. Only answers right first time count, and the timed arcade game is left out because the child is hurrying. It keeps a weekly summary for 12 weeks (one record per device, so two devices never double count) and a recent time for the last 300 words. The child sees nothing and stars and gems do not change. The grown-ups corner shows a plain trend and the slowest words. Code: `app/js/fluency.js` and the reading-speed part of `app/js/store.js`.
- **After World 7**: **daily practice** (the star button on the map once World 1 is finished) is a short mix (sounds, two hop words, heart words, speed read, memory, a sentence game, a spelling word) chosen from what is due in the spaced-repetition boxes across every finished world, ending with a small reward (10 gems for the first round of a day, 3 for later ones). It never runs out. Eight **big books** (`BOOKS` in `data.js`, 6 to 10 pages, 1 or 2 sentences a page, 3 questions each) open one at a time once every world is finished, each after the one before is read. The last lesson now ends on a screen that points to both.
- **Chicken-chicks theme**: Pip is the hero baby chick (`pipSVG` in `app/js/art.js`, same style as `babies.js`). The seven worlds keep their ids, lessons and saved progress but have chick-adventure names and scenery drawn in code (`app/js/scenery.js`, themes in `app/css/app.css`): Sunny Farm, Meadow Muddle, Duck Pond, Barn Loft, Cloud Coop, Seaside Shells, Starry Night Roost.
- **Bonus games** (`app/js/milestone.js`, `app/css/milestone.css`): finishing the last lesson of a world for the first time shows the egg hatch, then "World complete! Bonus game!" and that world's game: Chick Run (worlds 1, 4, 7), Feed the Chicks (2, 5), Hide and Seek (3, 6). Each star gives 5 gems; quitting with the cross still unlocks it but gives no gems. Every game is replayable from the Play corner with its best stars and score saved (`bonus` in the save, merge-safe: larger best, unlocked if either device unlocked). A world's game is also open for anyone who had already finished the world. Locked ones say "Finish World N!". Lines: `MS_LINES` in `data.js`. Test: `tests/milestone_flow.py` (flow) and `tests/milestone.py` (the games).
- **Rewards**: gems, 34 collectable baby chicks called Wordlings (one per lesson, all with decodable names; each has its own fluff colour, breed features and sometimes a small accessory, all drawn in code in `app/js/babies.js`; the one that has just hatched wears a bit of eggshell), hats for Pip, a library of finished books to re-read, and a Play corner (Bubble Blitz, Hop Read, Memory Match, plus the seven bonus games). There are no loot boxes or streak guilt, and nothing in the game limits how long a child can play (the grown-ups corner only shows the minutes played today).
- **Grown-ups corner**: hold the ⚙️ for about a second and a half. It shows the player and cloud-save buttons, progress (lessons done, recent accuracy, minutes played today, letters traced), a sound-mastery grid (tap to hear each sound), mix-ups and tricky words, understanding stories, reading speed, daily practice and big books, settings (background music, the Read it to Pip switch, skip-ahead, reset), how Wordwild teaches, and the credits.

## Tracing, reading aloud and calm mode
- **Letter tracing** (`app/js/trace.js`): after a new sound is heard and found, the child traces its letters with a finger. The letter is drawn large in Andika with a green start dot and a yellow guide ball. For a two- or three-letter sound (sh, igh) each letter is traced in turn, and the sound plays at the end. Stroke data for the 26 lowercase letters (start point, direction, stroke order) is in `LETTERS` at the top of the file. It is forgiving: the touch must start near the dot, the finger only has to stay roughly on the line, and after two failed tries the stroke is drawn for the child and the lesson carries on. A skip button is always on screen. It uses pointer events, so touch, pencil and mouse all work, and the page cannot scroll while a finger is on the letter. Each traced sound is counted in the saved progress (`trace`), merged safely with older saves and other devices. A sound is no longer traced after it has been traced three times. The stroke shapes follow a common Australian print style but are one person's reading of it, so check them against your school's handwriting guide.
- **Read it to Pip** (`app/js/readaloud.js`): an optional step, off by default, switched on in the grown-ups corner (which asks the browser for the microphone at that moment). After the first hop word of a lesson, and after the words of each book page are tapped, the child can tap the microphone, read the word or sentence aloud, hear their own voice, then hear the model reading. The lesson steps ask at most twice per lesson; book pages may ask once per page. The recording is made with `getUserMedia` and `MediaRecorder`, held in memory only, played once and thrown away. It is never saved, uploaded or sent to speech recognition. If the microphone is refused or missing, the step is skipped quietly and the child sees nothing. Pip's lines are `ra_*` in `PIP_LINES`.
- **Egg hatch**: after a lesson the egg wobbles for about one second, then cracks by itself (a tap makes it crack sooner), and the chick appears about 2 seconds after the screen. "Lesson complete!" plays while the egg wobbles and is never cut off. In reduced motion the egg glows instead of wobbling, with the same timing.
- **Books: tap every word.** On every story page (lesson stories, library re-reads and big books) the next button stays disabled until the child has tapped every word, left to right. The next word glows, tapped words stay highlighted, a word tapped out of order is only spoken and does not count, and the whole-sentence reading plays after the last word, which then opens the next button. The 🔊 button before then only says the next word. The back button still leaves the book; there is no way to skip a page. A word with no audio clip would still count (none are missing today). Code: `story()` in `app/js/games.js`.
- **Reading aloud in books**: with "Read it to Pip" on, the microphone is offered after each page's words are tapped (never required). If the child records, hears themselves and then the model, Pip cheers, one gem is given with a sparkle, and the page is counted (`reads` in saved progress, one count per device, merge-safe, shown in the grown-ups corner). Nothing is uploaded or stored and the microphone is released after each use. The lesson steps (hop word, sentence game) keep their limit; only book pages ask once per page.
- **Reduced motion**: when the device asks for reduced motion (iOS Settings, Accessibility, Motion), the big movements are calmed: no bouncing, shaking, flying gems or hopping chick, no confetti, no drifting or floating decorations, memory cards fade instead of flipping, bubbles drift more slowly. Hints, wrong answers and rewards stay clear through colour, glow and fades. In CSS this is one `prefers-reduced-motion` block at the end of `app/css/app.css`; in JavaScript `calm()` in `app/js/ui.js` does the same for effects, particles and bubbles.

## Layout
- `app/`: the static web app (no build step). `js/data.js` holds the curriculum, `js/trace.js` the letter tracing, `js/readaloud.js` Read it to Pip.
- `app/audio/`: voice clips. Words, sentences and Pip are synthesised by `tools/gen_audio.py` (Microsoft `en-AU-NatashaNeural`).
  The 62 pure letter sounds `p_*.mp3` are real recordings from Literacy Hub's "Phonemes and graphemes daily review" under **CC BY 4.0**. That licence is separate from the rest of Wordwild. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, the in-game **Credits & licences** screen (on the title screen and in the grown-ups corner), and `app/licences/literacyhub-audio-manifest.json` for each clip's slide, media file and edits.
- `tools/`
  - `build_content.mjs`: validate the curriculum, generate Pip's mistakes and the audio manifest
  - `gen_audio.py`: render the synthesised audio (skips `p_*`)
  - `import_literacyhub.py`: install the letter sounds from the unpacked slide pack (`python -I tools/import_literacyhub.py <unzipped pptx dir>`). `pure_sounds.py` (the old generated sounds) is retired and refuses to overwrite the recordings.
  - `pptx_inspect.py`: list a slide pack's slides, notes and audio relationships
  - `qa_audio.py`: Whisper/phoneme listening check
  - `make_audio_review.py`: writes `work/audio_review.html`, a double-click page to listen to the clips the listening check flagged and mark each right or wrong
  - `build_sw.mjs`: offline cache
  - `make_icons.py`: the app icons
- `tests/trace.py`: letter tracing in WebKit at iPhone and iPad sizes (all 26 letters, wrong start, two failed tries, multi-letter sounds, skip, lesson integration, saved progress and merging) plus the sound at the end in Chromium.
- `tests/readaloud.py`: Read it to Pip. Chromium with a fake microphone for the recording path (record, playback, model, nothing uploaded or stored, quota); WebKit for refused and missing microphone and the layout.
- `tests/hatch.py`: the egg cracks about 1 s after the hatch screen appears (normal and reduced motion, WebKit and Chromium), no spoken line cut off, all 34 chicks draw and differ, old saves keep their Wordlings, screenshots to `work/shots/babies/`.
- `tests/books.py`: tap-every-word in lesson stories, library re-reads and big books (WebKit iPhone and iPad), back button, every story word has a clip, Read it to Pip per page with a fake microphone in Chromium (offered, gem, nothing uploaded or stored, refused or off), merge-safe count.
- `tests/motion.py`: reduced motion in CSS and JavaScript (WebKit and Chromium), and that normal motion is unchanged.
- `tests/signin.py`: name and secret-code sign-in against a mocked cloud (nothing touches the real one).
- `tests/milestone_flow.py`: the bonus games wired into the lesson flow (World complete moment, gems, quit, Play corner, older saves, merging).
- `tests/play.py`: automated WebKit (Safari engine) playthrough at iPhone and iPad sizes, with screenshots saved to `work/shots/`.
- `tests/credits.py`: the letter-sound mapping (each sound plays its own clip; clips match the manifest) and the credits screen on iPhone/iPad.
- `tests/comprehension.py`: every story and big book has 2 or 3 sensible questions; playing them, saving the results, merging, old saves, the grown-ups line, layout at 375 px.
- `tests/fluency.py`: the timer (Chromium with real speech: voice and hidden time not counted), silly values, what each activity saves, nothing visible to the child, bounded and merge-safe history, the grown-ups trend.
- `tests/practice.py`: daily practice and big books (a save with only World 1 done, a save with everything done, a new save), the finished screen, one-at-a-time unlocking, layout, reduced motion, saved practice days.
- `tests/worlds.py`: plays chosen lessons from every world (all activity types) at iPhone and iPad sizes, plus the Memory Match arcade; screenshots to `work/shots/worlds/`.

## Players and cloud save
- On first launch the child (or a grown-up) types a **name**, then makes a **4-digit secret code**. Progress then saves to the cloud automatically.
- Typing the same name and code on another iPad or iPhone carries on from the same place. Progress from two devices is merged, so nothing earned is lost.
- If there's no internet, "Play without saving" keeps progress on the device. Cloud save can be switched on later from the grown-ups corner.
- Storage: the Whisker Academy Supabase project, in `ww_saves` / `ww_hits` (`cloud/schema.sql`). The tables are locked: the public key can only call the `ww_*` functions.
  - Unlike Whisker, the secret code is checked on the server: the game sends a hash of name + code, and 5 wrong tries lock the name for 30 seconds.
- Each launch adds one anonymous row to `ww_hits`: a random device id, no names.
- Monitoring: the **Kids** tab in Hermes Control (`Documents/Projects/hermes-control`, `api/kids.py`) shows plays and players for Whisker Academy and Wordwild. It's reachable on the phone over Tailscale at https://dell.tail80c7e7.ts.net:8445/hermes/.
